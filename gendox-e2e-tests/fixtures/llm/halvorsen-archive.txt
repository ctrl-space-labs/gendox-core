Halvorsen Project: archive notes

The Halvorsen project is a fictional research archive created for the automated tests of this application. Nothing in this document describes a real organization, place or person.

Access

The archive access code of the Halvorsen project is QUOKKA-4182. The code is given only to the archive keeper, who checks it before opening the reading room.

Holdings

The archive holds three collections: survey maps of a coastal valley, weather journals kept by the lighthouse staff, and letters between two botanists about salt-tolerant grasses. The maps are stored flat in drawers, the journals upright on open shelves, and the letters in acid-free boxes.

Opening hours

The reading room opens on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Visitors register at the front desk and may photograph documents without flash.
